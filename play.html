Choose from your Arsenal

The Sword — “Sharp, shiny, and comfortingly obvious. It slices, it dices, it solves problems in a very… direct way. A classic choice for those who like their solutions pointy.”
The Gun — “Heavy, loud, and a tad overdramatic. When you absolutely must make an impact—and possibly a mess—nothing shouts ‘problem solved’ quite like this.”
The Water Gun (filled with holy water) — “Plastic in shape, divine in payload. What looks like a child’s toy becomes a sacred sprinkler system of justice. Beware, foul creatures—hydration has never been so holy.”
The Heart Full of Hope — “It beats in your palm with a rhythm stronger than fear. Not made of flesh, but of belief. No blade, no bullet—just the outrageous audacity to keep going. A weapon softer than socks, yet heavier than despair.”
The Warm Fuzzy Socks — “Soft, squishy, and absurdly powerful. Slip them on, and suddenly the abyss feels like a couch and doom feels like a nap you can handle. They’re not enchanted—they’re just that cozy.”
The Rose — “Delicate petals, sneaky thorns. A mix of beauty and warning: admiration at a glance, pain at a squeeze. Wielded wisely, it may charm, distract, or at least smell nice while you’re in danger.”
